atomically, run a self-test, report bounded progress, and support cancellation, retry, status and
removal. Preserve digest/signature/SBOM/license/provenance checks and recover safely across
interruption, reconnect and concurrent requests. Keep all rendering and Ratatui integration in
the standalone asb-tui repository.
