This AR closes the concrete integration gap where a TUI fan-out action can appear
usable while emitting only agent/workload identifiers. The paired route must send
`kind`, `schema_version`, `idempotency_key`, agent/provider/model/workload IDs,
catalog/workload/scorer revisions, mode, cassette and credential-reference
digests, and bounded execution limits accepted by the ASB control server. Tests
must exercise the real serialized request contract as well as a deterministic
local fixture; fake backends alone are insufficient.

In the development prototype, absent credentials, signatures, or key-management
metadata are typed warnings and never block local/mock request validation or
offline qualification. Production trust hardening remains a separate concern.
