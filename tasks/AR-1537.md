# AR-1537

ASB's pre-v0.3.50 `handoffctl_vendor.py` allowlist predates the coordinator
runtime dependency closure. Syncing v0.3.50 therefore replaces the helper with
a verifier that imports modules not present in the target, leaving the state
tool unusable. Repair the downstream vendor bootstrap allowlist and its tests,
not the vendored coordinator runtime or its immutable manifest.

The repair must remain explicit, offline, path-confined and fail-closed. It may
only add coordinator paths that are present in the approved immutable release;
it must not accept arbitrary files, rewrite digests, or turn development
fixtures into formal AR-1307/1308 evidence.
