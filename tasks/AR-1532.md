# AR-1532

Repair the development-facing runner path associated with AR-1307. The path must remain provider-free and offline after installation, use only disposable locally generated seeds or local/mock inputs, enforce bounded argv-only execution and cleanup, and emit clearly labelled `qualification_authorized=false` evidence. Development must not require a reviewed seed, seed digest, signed bundle, publication digest, release authority, or native host. Formal/publication/release profiles and AR-1307's reviewed process envelope remain unchanged and are not qualified by this AR.

Audit the protected-main state runner and generated docs/tests for accidental development dependencies on formal seed/signature inputs. Repair only minimal state-owned implementation or contract gaps, and add positive and negative tests for generated-seed execution, missing formal inputs, timeout/cancellation cleanup, private evidence paths, and profile separation. Do not touch the ASB product, asb-tui, handoffctl implementation, or formal capacity contract. Record any remaining formal blocker as a separate handoff rather than weakening a gate.
