This AR is deliberately a development prototype. `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` may be
used as the live secret channel and generated local metadata may be used for
development authenticity. Missing credentials, signatures, or key-management
material are warning-only during setup and configuration; an explicitly chosen
live run must report a typed, actionable missing-key error rather than silently
falling back to mock execution. Production attestation and secret-management
hardening remain outside this AR.
