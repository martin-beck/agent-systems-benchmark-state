# AR-1527

Repair the coordination contract around AR-1307 and AR-1308. Development and
diagnostic execution must use freshly generated disposable seeds and locally
available inputs; no reviewed seed bytes, seed receipt, seed digest, signed
bundle, or publication digest may block that path. The resulting evidence must
remain explicitly non-qualifying (`qualification_authorized=false`).

Audit AR-1307, AR-1308, AR-1309, AR-1517--AR-1522 and all active dependents for
contradictory wording or dependency edges. Update only the development-path
requirements and next actions. Preserve formal/publication/release gates,
exact-input identity checks where they are needed for reproducibility, and the
truthful blocked status of full qualification. Add a generated-state check that
rejects a development dependency on a reviewed seed or digest while allowing
formal successors to retain their own explicit requirements.

This is state and documentation coordination work; do not modify asb-tui,
product runtime behavior, handoffctl, or formal models.
