# AR-1534

The merged AR-1530 capacity-profile change leaves `tools/handoffctl.py` different
from the immutable digest recorded by `coordinator.vendor.json`, so the required
vendor verification gate fails before AR-1307/1308 development repairs can be
published. Reconcile this as a coordinator-maintenance task only. Preserve the
vendor boundary: do not silently rewrite a digest, extract a replacement tool,
or weaken verification. Choose an approved vendor upgrade/patch path or relocate
the bounded state-view behavior outside the immutable vendor surface, with
independent review and exact evidence.

This AR owns no ASB product behavior, no asb-tui changes, no formal seed/input
substitution, and no release bypass. It must leave the coordinator's locking,
leases, generated views, privacy checks and fault coverage intact.
