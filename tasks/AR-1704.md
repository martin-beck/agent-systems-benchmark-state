This AR is a focused development repair and qualification boundary. It does
not claim that an HTTP error, missing key, or unavailable model is a successful
benchmark. Explicit live runs must surface bounded typed failures and never
silently fall back to mock or replay execution.

Development setup may use generated fixtures and missing authentication,
signatures, or key management remains visible and non-blocking. Production
trust and secret hardening remain fail-closed and outside this prototype AR.
