The runner must bind the selected agent, provider/model, workload, executable
digest, architecture, and replay mode before validating the content-addressed
experiment identity. A freshly generated plan must pass plan validation,
local/mock execution, selected-workload recording, strict offline replay, and
comparison. Stale or tampered experiment digests and mismatched executable or
workload identities must fail closed with typed diagnostics.

This is a functional development prototype: missing provider authentication,
signatures, and key management are visible development-only warnings and never
block local setup or qualification. No production secret or trust chain is
introduced by this AR.
