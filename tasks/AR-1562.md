Define a versioned channel selector for `asb tui install` and `upgrade`. The
selector must expose `dev`, `stable`, `nightly`, and `experimental`, default to
`dev` in the current development build, and reject unavailable non-dev channels
with a named error. The dev channel resolves the current ASB/asb-tui `main`
identities and is always labeled `development-only`; it must never be reported
as verified production support.

Add parser, schema, help, negative, and compatibility tests. Preserve the
existing signed production channel and ensure missing or malformed channel
metadata fails closed.
