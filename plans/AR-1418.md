# AR-1418: Tool-use reliability and safety workloads

## Objective

Represent the tool-use benchmarks documented in the literature review—especially
tau-bench's simulated-user reliability protocol and AgentDojo's tool-attack
robustness protocol—as selectable ASB workloads beside built-in software-engineering
tasks, while retaining their distinct graders and metrics.

## Dependencies and scope

- Depends on AR-1416 and AR-1408.
- Product repository only; `asb-tui` is out of scope.
- Owned paths: registry/catalog entries, tool-use workload adapters, local fixtures,
  reports, docs, and tests.

## Acceptance

1. Refresh the exact source, dataset/task, simulator, tool schema, policy/grader,
   license, and revision evidence for tau-bench and AgentDojo. If a source cannot
   be executed or legally qualified, keep a selectable inspection record that fails
   closed with a durable reason.
2. Add stable IDs and capability tags for simulated-user conversation, stateful
   tool use, adversarial tool-use attacks, and policy compliance. Never represent a
   framework or observability library as a workload.
3. Provide deterministic local/mock fixtures for repeated trials, pass^k/reliability,
   useful completion, policy violation, malformed tool calls, injected failures,
   reset, replay, and denied network. Keep utility, reliability, latency/cost, and
   safety violations in separate evidence fields and reports.
4. Ensure all selector, plan, run, record, replay, and report paths list these IDs
   beside built-in workloads, reject unknown/stale/duplicate identities, and reject
   attempts to pool incomparable metrics into one leaderboard.
5. Add positive and negative tests plus privacy/formal checks. Verify focused and
   full gates, exact-head CI, independent review, and all post-merge assurance runs.

## Non-goals

No live provider, simulated-user service, attack backend, network download, secret,
private transcript, native qualification, or weakened safety gate.
