## Diagnostic design

1. Preserve `TmuxServerObservationStage` inside startup server-before/server-after failures rather
   than collapsing it to unavailable. Render the result only from fixed context and stage labels.
2. Classify immediate `connect(2)` rejection and nonzero `SO_ERROR` into the same closed symbolic
   errno taxonomy: `refused`, `disappeared`, `permission`, `type_protocol`, `resource_transient`,
   and `unknown`. Never expose a numeric errno, socket path, PID, UID, session, credential, flags,
   raw output or timing.
3. Keep direct success and the already accepted incomplete `EINPROGRESS`/Unix `EAGAIN` paths exact.
   Every rejected class remains failure; do not broaden retries, delays, accepted errors or socket
   completion semantics.
4. Add exhaustive injected tests for every symbolic class at both immediate-connect and `SO_ERROR`
   boundaries, unknown positive/negative values, nested before/after propagation, label uniqueness,
   ASCII/length bounds, and absence of numeric/private content.
5. Preserve all owner/type/dev/inode socket bracketing, peer credentials, PID generation, stable
   pane/TTY identities, pre-option/pre-signal revalidation, process-group ownership and exact-server
   cleanup behavior byte-for-byte outside diagnostic typing.
