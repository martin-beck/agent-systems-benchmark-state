Branch: feature/kernel-diagnostics. Worktree: agent-systems-benchmark-kernel-diagnostics.

## Work sequence

1. Design capability probes and bounded diagnostics profiles.
2. Implement the smallest complete boundary and its independent failure tests.

observed outcomes and remaining limitations. No raw private configuration or transcripts.
Include negative cases and a real production-boundary integration where applicable.

outcome, review, exact-head checks and post-merge checks pass.
