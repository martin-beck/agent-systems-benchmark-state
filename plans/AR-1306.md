# AR-1306: Authenticated strict-replay fault-matrix qualification

## Outcome

Repair the missing real-service qualification identified in the independent review of PR #221.
The ASB fault matrix must exercise a runtime-owned `StrictReplayService` and relay listener, prove
authenticated positive request/response identity, and distinguish provider and descendant egress
denials from unrelated child failures on the approved isolated runner.

## Dependencies and ownership

This is a narrowly scoped follow-on to the blocked AR-1301 review and consumes the completed
runtime foundations AR-1300 and AR-1287. AR-1301 is the review predecessor whose PR #221 exposed
the gap; it is intentionally not a hard dependency so this AR can unblock AR-1301 rather than
forming a dependency cycle. Own ASB runtime/replay/CLI fixtures, schemas/docs and approved runner
evidence only. Do not modify asb-tui, handoffctl, or unrelated coordinator code.

## Work sequence

1. Start from protected ASB main and verify AR-1300 authenticated dispatch plus AR-1287 runner
   identity and capability attestations.
2. Implement a deterministic credential-free `StrictReplayService` and relay listener fixture that
   is created by the runtime-owned launch path, not by the test caller or a simulated log.
3. Add positive request/response identity and cassette parity assertions, including route, agent,
   run, attempt, generation and response digest bindings.
4. Add cause-specific provider-egress and descendant-egress denial fixtures, plus timeout,
   cancellation, crash, restart, cleanup/reaping, unrelated-process and no-fallback cases. Each
   case must prove its expected terminal cause and zero residual state.
5. Run the complete matrix repeatedly on the approved KVM runner, then focused/full locked ASB
   quality, privacy, formal and platform gates. Publish only a clean exact signed/DCO head, wait
   for exact-head CI, obtain independent review, and reconcile AR-1301 afterward.

## Acceptance and evidence

- The positive case receives an authenticated strict-replay response from the real runtime-owned
  service/listener and proves exact identity/parity; caller-created listeners and unqualified
  mocks do not count.
- Provider and descendant egress denials are separately observable and cannot be satisfied by an
  unrelated startup or child failure.
- Every lifecycle/fault case is executed through the approved delegated runner, has a bounded
  terminal result, and leaves no child, socket, lease, queue or scratch residual.
- Evidence binds candidate, runner, service/listener fixture, cassette, containment and exact
  matrix result without credentials, private paths, logs or transcripts.
- Signed commits, DCO, generated schemas/docs, focused tests, full applicable gates, exact-head
  CI, independent review, protected merge and post-merge verification all pass.
