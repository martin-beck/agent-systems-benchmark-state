Provide the mandatory credential-free agent-by-workload campaign path as an
ASB-owned successor to the stale local portion of AR-1333. The campaign must
use the runtime-owned deterministic mock, persist bounded per-tuple evidence,
support interruption/restart reconciliation, and replay the sealed matrix
strictly offline with deterministic comparison output.

Dependencies: AR-1332 (record/replay workflow), AR-1433 (runtime mock-attempt
backend), and AR-1447 (ASB local campaign qualification). AR-1329 and all
live-provider relay/authority ARs remain optional production successors and
must not block this AR, CI, or local completion. Do not modify asb-tui.

2. Verify the complete selected agent × workload matrix through the local/mock

3. Reject missing, duplicate, stale, mismatched, credential-bearing, external,
   or unsealed tuple inputs before any execution effect.
4. Exercise interruption, cancellation, restart reconciliation, strict
   offline replay, comparison, privacy scanning, and network-denial tests.
5. Regenerate documentation/examples and publish signed+DCO changes through
   independent review, exact-head CI, merge, and all seven post-merge gates.

digest evidence, replays without provider contact, and yields deterministic
comparison output. AR-1329 remains clearly labeled as optional production/live
