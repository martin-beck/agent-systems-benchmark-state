# AR-1257: Qualify digest-pinned multiarch MockAgents image

## Outcome

Provision and qualify an official digest-pinned multiarch OCI image capable of running the
credential-free ASB MockAgents transport under the pinned QEMU AArch64 lane. Verify immutable
provenance, signature, license, executable contents, network isolation, bounded transport, and
reproducible positive/negative evidence.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-1250, AR-1251, AR-1233.
Owned paths: `tools/mockagents/`, `docs/MOCKAGENTS_QEMU.md`, and a versioned public support
matrix under `platforms/v1/`. Do not modify asb-tui or production provider mappings.

## Acceptance

- Image reference is immutable by OCI digest, with verified signature, source revision, license,
  architecture manifests, and bounded SBOM/provenance.
- QEMU AArch64 and x86_64 runs use argv-only bounded transport, loopback-only networking, no
  credentials or ambient configuration, and retain only sanitized evidence.
- Positive request/response, malformed/oversized/unknown-field, timeout/cancellation, stale
  generation, egress-denial, and cleanup tests pass on every advertised architecture.
- Missing registry/image/signature/QEMU capability remains explicitly unsupported; no mutable tag,
  compatible substitute, or skipped test may establish support.

If the official artifact or required verification capability is unavailable, release this AR
blocked with exact public provenance and no product claim.
