# AR-1007: Maintain benchmark validity and portability registry

## Outcome

Track dataset provenance, contamination risk, grader validity and native portability per workload revision.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0401, AR-0701, AR-1001.
Owned paths: asb-workloads registry, provenance and validation.
Branch: feature/benchmark-validity. Worktree: agent-systems-benchmark-benchmark-validity.
One worker owns this task; shared workspace/schema edits require coordinator integration.

## Work sequence

1. Implement registry schema and validation for built-in and imported workloads.
2. Implement the smallest complete boundary and its independent failure tests.
3. Run focused and applicable full quality gates on the exact commit.
4. Publish a focused PR, reconcile CI and record verified limitations.

## Acceptance criteria

Registry records source/license/hash/date/split/baseline/dependencies/limitations; holdout policy and exposure metadata explicit; reference patches pass on every claimed native platform; architecture adaptations are labelled; speedup thresholds are recalibrated per host with uncertainty.

## Evidence and completion

Record source/tool versions, commands by digest, immutable commit and CI identifiers,
observed outcomes and remaining limitations. No raw private configuration or transcripts.
Include negative cases and a real production-boundary integration where applicable.
Do not satisfy native evidence with mocks or emulation. Finish only after the requested
outcome, review, exact-head checks and post-merge checks pass.
