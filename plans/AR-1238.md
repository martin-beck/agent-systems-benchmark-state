# AR-1238: Runtime-owned loopback supervisor

## Outcome

Provide a reviewed in-tree supervisor that composes the strict-replay sidecar and adapter inside a
private user/network namespace with only loopback access to the authenticated cassette relay.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-1100 and AR-1231. Repository: `martin-beck/agent-systems-benchmark`. Own only
asb-runtime supervisor/namespace composition, relay FD or socket handoff, readiness attestation,
and native lifecycle/isolation tests.

## Acceptance

- Private user/network namespace with only the supervisor-owned loopback interface enabled.
- Per-launch `0600` Unix relay mounted or handed into the namespace; no host-network sharing.
- Pinned/in-tree supervisor command identity and authenticated route/generation binding.
- Sidecar and adapter start as one bounded unit with complete teardown and descendant reaping.
- Provider/external egress denial and unrelated-process non-interference are proven.
- Fail closed when user-namespace or namespace setup is unavailable; preserve `NetworkPolicy::Deny`.
- Signed DCO, locked quality/privacy/policy/native gates, exact-head review/CI, post-merge checks.
