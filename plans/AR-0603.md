# AR-0603: Establish pinned CSB execution and conformance boundary

## Outcome

Provide the independently auditable provenance and execution-safety foundation required before
the optional ASB-to-CSB bridge may invoke any CSB program.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0101, AR-0102, AR-0103, AR-0104.
Owned paths: a new asb-csb-runner boundary and its tests, bounded public fixtures under
tests/fixtures/csb-runner, and docs/CSB_PROVENANCE.md. Root Cargo, lockfile, schema, SBOM, and
release registration are coordinator-serialized fences. Do not edit the production monitoring
mapping owned by AR-0602.
Branch: feature/csb-execution-assurance. Worktree:
agent-systems-benchmark-csb-execution-assurance.

## Work sequence

1. Acquire under /srv/data/projects and pin the official source URL, tag, commit, tree, license
   files, modules, submodules, toolchain, build inputs, and executable digests. Prove an offline
   reproducible build or record the exact residual limit.
2. Define a version-negotiated length-bounded subprocess contract for arguments, configuration,
   environment, working directory, frames, stdout/stderr, exits, signals, causal IDs, and
   artifacts. Do not use a Rust dylib ABI or a shell command boundary.
3. Run only through AR-0102/AR-0103 process-tree and cgroup containment with absolute deadlines,
   bounded output/backpressure, explicit ownership, cancellation, descendant cleanup, and
   conservative NeedsReconciliation recovery.
4. Enforce disjoint trusted workspace/state/artifact roots, no-follow path handling, bounded
   regular artifacts, atomic AR-0104 commits, immutable executable/source re-verification, and an
   allowlisted environment with no ambient credentials, configuration, or network.
5. Keep CSB opt-in and absent from the default ASB dependency/build/runtime graph; exclude
   generator and syzkaller coupling.

## Acceptance criteria

- Exact public source and complete build/license provenance are independently verified and bound
  to the executed bytes.
- Unknown versions, duplicate or out-of-order IDs, malformed/oversized/truncated traffic, floods,
  slow peers, cancellation races, process escape, crash windows, partial artifacts, path attacks,
  replacement races, and cleanup failures are bounded and fail closed.
- Every external effect has a durable pre-effect intent; retries are idempotent, uncertain effects
  remain NeedsReconciliation, and repeated runs leave no process, cgroup, lease, staging file, or
  private-data residue.
- A real pinned credential-free offline fixture passes natively on Linux x86_64. aarch64 or other
  platform support is claimed only after native containment evidence; Windows, macOS, musl,
  privileged modes, arbitrary plugins/network, generators, and syzkaller remain unsupported until
  separately qualified.

## Evidence and completion

Record immutable source/build/artifact digests, license and toolchain evidence, protocol fixtures,
fault barriers, native process/cgroup audits, privacy scans, focused/full/formal checks, immutable
review, exact-head CI, and post-merge recovery. Publish no credentials, prompts/responses,
transcripts, host paths, raw private logs, or unrelated CSB history.
