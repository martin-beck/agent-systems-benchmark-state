# AR-1327: OpenRouter adapter projections and parity conformance

## Outcome

Every compatible ASB agent adapter launches through the AR-1325 OpenRouter
profile with identical effective model/settings/endpoint projections and the
`OPENROUTER_API_KEY` credential target, and cross-agent parity is proven by the
AR-0315 conformance harness with hostile fail-closed evidence.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0315 (provider parity conformance, done), AR-1325 (OpenRouter
profile), and AR-1326 (CLI catalog/selection). Owned paths: per-agent adapter
translations, the provider-aware launch projection (`provider_launch.rs`,
`openai.rs` translation surface), credential-target mapping, egress allowance
lists, parity fixtures, and the support matrix. Branch:
`feature/ar-1327-openrouter-adapter-parity`. Worktree:
`agent-systems-benchmark-ar-1327-openrouter-adapter-parity`.

## Work sequence

1. Translate the OpenRouter profile through every compatible agent's adapter
   projection; the constructor-controlled projection must equal every provider,
   model, API-mode, settings, and resolver field in the requested launch.
2. Map the credential target to `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` per agent and confirm the
   environment resolver never exposes the secret in argv, environment, logs,
   manifests, or errors.
3. Audit and extend known-egress allowances to `openrouter.ai` explicitly for
   every compatible adapter; never add a silent fallback.
4. Capture redacted effective requests at each real agent boundary and compare
   normalized and wire-effective fields, tool schemas, streaming, retries,
   deadlines, and cancellation across agents.
5. Run hostile cases: absent credentials, endpoint mismatch, unsupported
   settings, malformed responses, parallel same-profile sessions, credential
   bleed, and replay/live confusion; every negative case fails closed.
6. Publish the exact supported/unsupported agent-provider-platform matrix and
   all evidence limits.

## Acceptance criteria

Every support claim has a real pinned-agent boundary test; identical means all
requested and observable effective fields match, not merely that runs complete;
`OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is the only secret channel; unsupported or unobservable
settings remain unsupported; replay and live cannot be confused; and native and
platform limits are explicit.

## Evidence and completion

Record immutable commits, upstream pins/licenses, redacted structural request
evidence, negative/fault results, independent review, exact-head CI, native
runs, and post-merge verification.
