Run the pinned executable against a credential-free loopback provider and prove editing, tools, usage, cancellation, cleanup, and network denial.

## Series-wide safety and evidence contract

Use only the official immutable source/package/license identity recorded by the provenance phase.
No consumer account or credential is required: live evidence uses stable public sentinels and a
loopback fake provider, clears ambient environment/configuration, isolates mode-0700 state and
workspace roots, denies non-loopback network, and records no raw prompt, credential, authorization
header, host-private path, user name, or machine identifier. Events, actions, turns, requests,
responses, output, retries, deadlines, and cleanup are bounded. Capabilities are registered only at
an observable consumer API; buffered outcomes are not called live streaming.

## Phase acceptance

The exact provenance-pinned executable completes a credential-free loopback journey that makes an isolated graded edit, emits causal lifecycle/tool and positive internally consistent usage evidence, reaches one terminal result, survives cancellation races, reaps its process tree, and cannot reach non-loopback network. Tests reject tool/error/terminal corruption, retry exhaustion, trickled output, ambient credentials/config, root overlap, child escape, and private-data sentinels.

Run phase-focused positives and hostile negatives plus all affected workspace format, Clippy,
tests, docs, release, dependency/license/SBOM, privacy/Gitleaks, replay, fault/formal, and applicable
native x86_64/aarch64 gates. Publish only an independently reviewed SSH-signed+DCO exact tree.

## Unsupported and blocking limits

This phase alone does not qualify OpenJiuwen. Mocks, parser fixtures, source inspection, compilation,
cross-builds, version output, or replay without the pinned live executable do not establish support.
Do not infer untested providers, models, plugins, autonomous permissions, platforms, architectures,
external services, or telemetry behavior. If official executable source/package/license closure or
any required live/replay evidence is unavailable, block this child with the exact reason; do not
substitute a compatible-looking SDK, mutable distribution, private credential, or weakened check.
The other agent series remains independent, and umbrella AR-0856 cannot complete until both final
qualification children are done.
