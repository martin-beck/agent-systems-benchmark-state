# AR-1331: Runtime-owned strict-replay launch authority

## Outcome

`asb replay` runs strict replay of sealed cassettes through a
`ReplayLaunchAuthority` that is issued only by the runtime, is injected through
the supervised context the CLI already requires, and cannot be fabricated by a
caller-supplied manifest, path, or CLI flag. Replay stays strict, offline-only,
and without any live-provider fallback.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-1330 (sealed cassettes from live capture). Owned paths: the
runtime-owned authority construction and lifecycle, the replay CLI binding,
and the strict-replay supervision context in the ASB product. Branch:
`feature/ar-1331-runtime-replay-launch-authority`. Worktree:
`agent-systems-benchmark-ar-1331-runtime-replay-launch-authority`.

## Work sequence

1. Define the runtime-owned authority value that binds a live run context to the
   sealed cassettes and carries the exact route and cassette digest set.
2. Construct and inject the authority through the supervised context that
   `replay` already requires, replacing the current unconditional rejection.
3. Prove by negative tests that a user-supplied manifest, path, or flag cannot
   synthesize the authority or bypass the runtime.
4. Keep replay strict: no provider fallback, no fabricated cassettes, no
   weakening of the offline default.
5. Add replay tests: authority injection, fabrication rejection, missing
   authority, digest mismatch, and offline behavior with sealed evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

Replay executes only with a runtime-issued authority bound to the exact sealed
cassettes; no caller input can fabricate or replace it; replay never contacts a
provider; and the fail-closed CLI contract from the replay work is preserved.

## Evidence and completion

Record immutable commits, fabrication negative fixtures, replay lifecycle
evidence, independent review, exact-head CI, and post-merge verification.
