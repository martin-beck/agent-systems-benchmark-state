# AR-1001: Define experiment identity and comparability

## Outcome

Make every comparison content-addressed and explicit about agent, model, workload and platform confounders.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0101, AR-0203.
Owned paths: asb-core/asb-protocol experiment manifests and asb-analysis comparison.
Branch: feature/experiment-comparability. Worktree: agent-systems-benchmark-experiment-comparability.
One worker owns this task; shared workspace/schema edits require coordinator integration.

## Work sequence

1. Implement the experiment identity model and comparison compatibility rules.
2. Implement the smallest complete boundary and its independent failure tests.
3. Run focused and applicable full quality gates on the exact commit.
4. Publish a focused PR, reconcile CI and record verified limitations.

## Acceptance criteria

Manifest records agent binary, model/provider/settings, tool policy, workload/scorer revisions, image/dependency hashes, kernel/distro/architecture, CPU/NUMA/governor, cache/load/retry/replay settings; compare lists differences and refuses unqualified claims for required mismatches; matched and confounded fixtures pass.

## Evidence and completion

Record source/tool versions, commands by digest, immutable commit and CI identifiers,
observed outcomes and remaining limitations. No raw private configuration or transcripts.
Include negative cases and a real production-boundary integration where applicable.
Do not satisfy native evidence with mocks or emulation. Finish only after the requested
outcome, review, exact-head checks and post-merge checks pass.
