# AR-1294: Restore state formal-gate baseline integrity

Repair the state-repository baseline failures that prevent review of AR-1293.
The coordinator vendor lock must describe the exact checked-in vendor payload,
while local formal admission must use a bounded, owner-safe lock path rather
than a stale root-owned shared file.

## Scope and decision gate

Audit the coordinator release provenance and the documentation change that made
the recorded digest stale. Choose either a verified vendor resynchronization
from the exact immutable upstream release or a clean separation of local
documentation from vendored files; never silently rewrite a digest. Audit the
TLC admission lock lifecycle and make its default/configuration deterministic,
private to the invoking worker, and safe for concurrent jobs. Do not chmod,
delete, or take ownership of another service's lock, and do not modify
handoffctl implementation or ASB product sources.

## Acceptance

- `handoffctl_vendor.py verify` passes against an immutable, documented vendor
  identity and all existing vendor-negative tests remain effective.
- Formal smoke can acquire its own lock or fails with a clear bounded diagnostic;
  stale/root-owned locks cannot cause cross-user interference or false success.
- Full state tests, formal smoke, privacy, signature/DCO, and exact review gates
  pass, or the remaining external capability is recorded precisely.
