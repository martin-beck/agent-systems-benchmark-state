# AR-1262: Runtime-owned strict-replay launch authority

## Outcome

Provide a runtime-owned, one-shot authority issuer that atomically creates the authenticated
relay handoff, denied sandbox launch context, resource lease, generation/deadline, and verified
supervisor/sidecar commands consumed by strict-replay CLI execution.

## Dependencies and ownership

Depends on AR-1237, AR-1238, and AR-1239. Preserve AR-1261 as the reviewed predecessor and do
not duplicate its CLI contract. Owned paths are `crates/asb-runtime/src/replay_entrypoint.rs`,
its narrow export, runtime entrypoint tests, and the corresponding CLI consumer wiring/tests.
Do not modify asb-tui, provider catalogs, campaign code, or existing supervisor/bundle logic.

## Acceptance

- Runtime issues opaque authenticated `ReplayRelayHandoff`, `SandboxLaunchInput`, `ResourceLease`,
  generation/deadline, deny policy, and verified supervisor/sidecar command identities.
- The capability is one-shot and rejects forged, stale, duplicate, route/digest-mismatched, or
  expired inputs; CLI cannot create readiness, routes, leases, commands, or arbitrary paths.
- CLI invokes `StrictReplayLaunchBridge::spawn` through the runtime capability, and a real cassette
  request/response proves supervised execution and provider/descendant egress denial.
- Timeout, cancellation, crash, restart, partial launch, duplicate attempt, and no-fallback paths
  reap children and clean relay sockets, routes, leases, and state.
- Focused/full locked tests, fmt, Clippy, rustdoc, policy/privacy/formal/portability gates,
  signed DCO review, exact-head CI, protected merge, and post-merge verification pass.
