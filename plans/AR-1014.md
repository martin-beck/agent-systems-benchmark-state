AR-0804 and AR-0805 are read-only migration and reviewed-behavior inputs; they do not reopen any
legacy ASB UI path or transfer implementation ownership out of the standalone repository.
Repository: `martin-beck/asb-tui`. Consume the reviewed ASB catalog through the standalone TUI
protocol. Implement a visible search field plus grouped selection with tri-state group counts,
select-all/clear-group, drill-down and independent per-measure selection/deselection. Include
validation, presets, resize-safe rendering, deterministic plan serialization, explicit units,
support/overhead reasons and focused negative tests before exact-head CI. Assert exact unchecked,
partially selected and fully selected group counts and transitions, plus byte-stable exported plan
fixtures that round-trip through ASB validation.
The TUI must submit the exact AR-1037 canonical selection and show mandatory non-selectable evidence
separately; it may not imply that mandatory outcome, scoring or provenance evidence can be disabled.
