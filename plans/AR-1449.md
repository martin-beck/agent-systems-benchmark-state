# AR-1449: Runtime-owned local replay CLI authority

Implement a runtime-owned local replay authority acquisition path for the ordinary ASB CLI. The path must consume only validated cassette identity and runtime-owned sandbox, lease, relay and attestation objects; callers must not construct authority. Integrate the one-shot authority into the guided wrapper and prove strict offline replay, cancellation, teardown, mismatch denial and path safety.

Dependencies: AR-1443 and AR-1448. Repository: martin-beck/agent-systems-benchmark. Do not touch asb-tui.
