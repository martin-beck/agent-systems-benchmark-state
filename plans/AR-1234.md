# AR-1234: Runtime-owned loopback namespace capability

## Outcome

Provide an approved, runtime-owned mechanism for a sandboxed strict-replay child to reach only
its authenticated loopback cassette service while provider and descendant egress remain denied.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-1100 and AR-1231. Repository: `martin-beck/agent-systems-benchmark`. Own only
asb-runtime sandbox namespace provisioning and its native qualification fixtures.

## Acceptance

- Add a typed backend capability distinct from `NetworkPolicy::Deny` and `Host`; no host-network
  sharing, ambient `ip` commands, credentials, or unapproved privileged helpers.
- Provision/configure loopback through an immutable runtime-owned helper or kernel-backed seam,
  fail closed when unavailable, and bind listener address plus route digest to the launch record.
- Prove a real child reaches its owned cassette service, while provider and descendant connection
  attempts are denied; cover timeout, cancellation, restart, stale generation, duplicate attempt,
  cleanup, and partial-launch outcomes.
- Preserve existing Deny behavior and schemas; add privacy-safe docs, positive and negative tests,
  full locked/policy/signature/native gates, and handoff evidence for AR-1233.

## Explicit non-goals

No host network sharing, ambient shell/ip setup, provider discovery, credential handling, or TUI.
