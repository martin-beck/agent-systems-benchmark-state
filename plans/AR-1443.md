# AR-1443 — Guided benchmark, offline capture, and comparison

## Scope

Provide one guided command/route after setup that lets a user select workloads
and agents, run a bounded benchmark, optionally capture responses for every
selected tuple, switch to strict offline replay, and compare results with clear
provider/model/agent/workload identities and readiness labels.

## Dependencies

Depends on AR-1442, AR-1332, AR-1333, and AR-1437. It composes existing
record/replay and campaign contracts; it must not weaken live-provider authority
or make external access a CI dependency.

## Acceptance

- The normal path is selection-driven: no hand-authored manifest is required
  for the supported workload/agent matrix.
- Live capture is explicit, bounded, resumable, and produces sealed cassettes;
  replay is strictly offline and fails closed on missing or mismatched tuples.
- Results comparison explains unavailable, simulated, replayed, and live
  evidence separately and prevents mixed-provider/model comparisons.
- The same campaign identity and result schema are rendered by CLI and TUI.

## Required evidence

Deterministic local mock campaign, hostile mismatch/offline tests, comparison
fixtures, exact-head hosted gates, and a disposable clean-install-to-report
journey.
