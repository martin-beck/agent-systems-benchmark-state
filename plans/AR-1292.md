# AR-1292: Repair pinned TLA+ artifact provenance

Resolve the fail-closed upstream provenance mismatch blocking AR-0704/PR #119.
The official TLA+ v1.8.0 asset currently has the expected byte count but a
different digest from the repository pin. Preserve the formal gate and do not
accept a replacement until its source, version, byte count, digest, signature
or equivalent independent provenance, and reproducible hosted acquisition are
verified.

## Scope and decision gate

Audit the current asset and all repository pins without changing product behavior.
If the upstream release is authentic and the repository's pin is stale, update
only the exact version-bound provenance fixture and its positive/negative tests
on a signed current-main branch. If authenticity cannot be established, leave
the gate blocked and document the external evidence required. Never accept a
hash merely because a download changed, disable formal tests, or broaden the
accepted artifact set.

## Acceptance

- The exact old/new bytes, source URL, version, size, digest, and provenance
  decision are recorded without private paths or raw credentials.
- Any product change is minimal, signed, DCO-bearing, independently reviewed,
  exact-head green, and protected-merged; malformed, wrong-version, wrong-size,
  wrong-hash, and failed-download cases remain rejected.
- PR #119 is requalified only after all required checks and post-merge assurance
  are terminal green. Otherwise AR-0704 remains blocked with a precise external
  blocker and no gate weakening.
- No asb-tui source, runtime dependency, native-host requirement, or unrelated
  formal behavior is changed.
