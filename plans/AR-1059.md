Make AR-1024 executable only after its stale candidate is rebased onto the final measurement
selection contract and the independently built asb-tui artifact has passed trusted-main
qualification. Require the ASB router to preserve only validated terminal context, clean up every
child on every post-spawn failure and reject daemonization or ownership escape without taking
ownership of any UI implementation.

## Privacy-safe terminal context

The delegated candidate starts from `env_clear`. Forward paths only through the existing
owner-safe absolute XDG/HOME validation. Forward `TERM` and `COLORTERM` only as nonempty,
bounded ASCII terminal tokens; invalid values fail before spawn. Forward presence-only context such
as `NO_COLOR`, `TMUX`, `STY`, `SSH_CONNECTION` and `SSH_TTY` only as documented fixed
boolean sentinels, never caller values. Do not forward arbitrary locale, shell, display, proxy,
credential, session, socket, endpoint or provider variables. Terminal descriptors remain inherited
only where the closed launch operation requires them.

Tests must prove exact allowlisting, empty/overlong/non-ASCII/control/separator rejection, normalized
boolean presence, absence preservation and secret-marker non-reflection in argv, environment,
responses, errors and logs. Local TTY, SSH, tmux, GNU Screen, plain and NO_COLOR fixtures must
observe the same safe classifications as direct standalone execution without receiving raw socket,
session or endpoint text.

## Post-spawn cleanup

Install an armed RAII child/process-group guard immediately after every successful external spawn,
before serialization, pipe extraction, write, reader setup, wait or decode. Apply the same closed
cleanup contract to candidate execution, transfer helpers and signature verification. Every return,
error and panic after spawn must close owned pipes, terminate only the identity-checked owned
process or process group, join/drain bounded readers, reap every owned child and preserve the
original closed public error. Disarm only after confirmed exit/reap and bounded output validation.

Inject failures at every post-spawn seam: request serialization, missing stdin/stdout, short/blocked
write, reader creation/read/timeout, over-limit output, wait/status, malformed response, response/
exit disagreement, transfer failure and verifier failure. Each fixture must prove bounded
completion, zero zombies/descendants, no leaked descriptors or reader threads, unchanged active
installation and no signal to the authoritative benchmark runner or unrelated processes.

## Foreground and no-daemon ownership boundary

A lifecycle candidate is a foreground child in one ASB-owned process group. It may not daemonize,
double-fork, create a new session/process group, retain lifecycle pipes after its leader exits or
leave descendants after a bounded operation. A launched interactive frontend remains the observed
foreground lifecycle process; benchmark runners stay independently authoritative and outside the
frontend cleanup group.

Exercise direct exit, same-group descendants, descendant-held pipes, `setsid`, double-fork plus
`setsid`, leader exit, PID reuse, concurrent unrelated same-owner children and transient as well
as persistent process-observation failure. Any ownership ambiguity or escape fails closed and
blocks activation/publication. Cleanup must never classify, signal, wait for or reap an unrelated
process. If the supported rootless platform cannot prove this boundary without cgroup or equivalent
authority, reject that launch mode explicitly rather than claiming daemon cleanup.
