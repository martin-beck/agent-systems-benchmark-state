# AR-1415: Total literature workload selector coverage

## Objective

Turn the complete literature workload inventory into an auditable selector
contract beside the seven built-in software-engineering fixtures. Every record
must be discoverable by stable ID and family, but unavailable records must stay
unavailable until their own evidence gates pass.

## Dependencies

- AR-1410 (literature selector completeness)
- AR-1414 (action-pin follow-up and current generated-state baseline)

## Acceptance

- A generated parity check covers every workload named in `docs/WORKLOADS.md`,
  `docs/RELATED_WORK.md`, and the registry, including AgentBench, tau-bench,
  AgentDojo, SWE-bench variants, Terminal-Bench, Aider Polyglot, BigCodeBench,
  EvalPlus, LiveCodeBench, SWE-Lancer, SWE-rebench, SWE-Perf, SWEfficiency, and
  CORE-Bench; framework-only entries are explicitly classified rather than
  silently omitted.
- Built-in and literature IDs are emitted together by catalog/doctor and can be
  selected by family/capability/evidence state without aliases that collide or
  silently change identity.
- Missing license, evaluator, image, reset, contamination-window, or platform
  evidence keeps a record visibly unavailable; plan and run reject it before any
  network, provider, or privileged-host operation.
- Positive and negative tests cover omission, duplicate/stale digest, unknown
  selector, unavailable selection, and generated-doc drift.

## Verification

Run focused registry/catalog/CLI tests, generated-doc and schema parity, full
workspace quality gates, exact-head CI, independent review, and all required
post-merge assurance workflows.
