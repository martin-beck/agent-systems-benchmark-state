# AR-1330: Live provider capture into a sealed cassette

## Outcome

Every real provider exchange from an AR-1329 live run is captured through the
existing `StrictReplayService::capture_authenticated_connection` seam,
sanitized and redacted, sealed as a content-addressed `CassetteContents`, and
recorded with its digest and verification result. No credential, prompt,
response, or raw capture data enters durable state or public control responses,
and interrupted captures reconcile without repeating paid work.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0502 (replay cassettes), AR-0503 (strict replay), and AR-1329
(live run execution), all done/planned as recorded. Owned paths: the capture
integration in the live run path, the `Redactor` invocation, cassette sealing
and digesting, the per-tuple capture record, and interruption reconciliation in
the ASB product. Branch: `feature/ar-1330-live-capture-sealed-cassette`.
Worktree: `agent-systems-benchmark-ar-1330-live-capture-sealed-cassette`.

## Work sequence

1. Invoke `capture_authenticated_connection` from the live execution path with
   the runtime-issued route and bounded request/response exchange only.
2. Run the existing `Redactor` over the exchange, seal the result as
   `CassetteContents`, and compute its content address before any persistence.
3. Persist per-tuple intent before provider effects and reconcile interrupted
   `in_progress` tuples to `stale` without replaying uncertain paid work.
4. Record the cassette digest, redaction result, and verification result for
   every tuple; reject duplicates, missing digests, and identity mismatches.
5. Add capture tests: positive seal, redaction of secrets, malformed exchange,
   interruption/restart, duplicate rejection, and privacy scanning of durable
   state.

## Acceptance criteria

The capture callback is reached only through the runtime-owned live path and
cannot be bypassed or supplied by a frontend; sealed cassettes are
content-addressed and privacy-safe; interrupted tuples never repeat uncertain
provider effects; and offline replay remains separate and without fallback.

## Evidence and completion

Record immutable commits, redacted sealed-cassette observations, digest
vectors, interruption evidence, independent review, exact-head CI, and
post-merge verification.

## Current development and CI qualification boundary

The mandatory development and CI qualification path for this AR is a deterministic
local provider/LLM mock (LiteLLM-compatible where practical), including hostile
negative tests and offline replay where applicable. External/live OpenRouter or
other provider reachability is optional supplementary evidence only; it is never a
completion, dependency-readiness, or CI gate. Production egress policy, credential
non-disclosure, runtime-owned authority, namespace/relay attestation, cancellation
and teardown, and fail-closed denial of unapproved external traffic remain required
contracts. Existing live-provider dependency edges describe production integration
ordering only and must not be used to block local qualification or to claim external
reachability.
