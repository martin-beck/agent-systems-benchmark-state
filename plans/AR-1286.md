# AR-1286: Supervised strict-replay cassette lifecycle

## Outcome

Consume the merged runtime-owned launch factory and authenticated replay transport to execute the
primary strict-replay cassette path under bounded supervision. This is ASB-only and excludes all
asb-tui, renderer, terminal, and frontend paths.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-1282, AR-1285, AR-1237, AR-1238, and AR-1239. All are complete and merged; do
not reuse blocked predecessor branches. Owned paths are `crates/asb-runtime`, `crates/asb-replay`,
`crates/asb-cli`, and bounded integration fixtures/tests for the runtime-to-CLI lifecycle.
Branch: `feature/ar-1286-supervised-replay-lifecycle`; worktree:
`agent-systems-benchmark-ar-1286-supervised-replay-lifecycle`.

## Acceptance

- The actual argument-level strict-replay command receives a runtime-issued launch context and
  executes a real bounded child/cassette request-response exchange; audit-only or metadata-only
  replay is insufficient.
- Provider and descendant egress are denied by the qualified sandbox boundary, with no live
  fallback. The test records only bounded, privacy-safe typed outcomes and provenance.
- Cancellation, timeout, crash, restart, cleanup, malformed, stale, duplicate, closed-channel,
  and unrelated-process non-interference cases are executable and fail closed.
- Child/process ownership, deadlines, resource leases, request identity, generation, and cleanup
  are validated at every boundary; no caller-created authority, ambient host data, credentials,
  runtime downloads, private paths, or unbounded subprocess output may enter the contract.
- Add positive and negative fixtures for each new behavior, preserve public schemas and unknown
  field rejection, and run focused/full locked tests, fmt, Clippy, rustdoc, policy, formal,
  privacy, and portability gates.
- Obtain independent exact-head review, signed SSH+DCO PR CI, protected signed merge, and all
  exact-main post-merge workflows before release.

If an attested sandbox capability or runtime-issued child service is unavailable, record the exact
fail-closed blocker and split a narrower successor rather than fabricating authority or weakening
the egress/lifecycle claims.
