# AR-1237: Strict-replay child launch bridge

## Outcome

Map the authenticated `ReplayRelayHandoff` and approved `LoopbackOnly` capability into a real
strict-replay child launch while preserving denied provider/ambient egress.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-1233 and AR-1236. Repository: `martin-beck/agent-systems-benchmark`. Own only the
strict-replay launch/adapter contract and its integration tests; do not modify TUI or unrelated
provider lifecycle work.

## Acceptance

- Versioned launch metadata binds route digest, socket path, child endpoint, generation, and deadline.
- Sidecar/adapter lifecycle is supervised and fails closed on stale, duplicate, partial, or failed launch.
- Real child cassette success with provider/descendant denial and complete cleanup tests.
- No credentials or external network; bounded privacy-safe evidence only.
- Signed DCO, locked quality/privacy/policy/native gates, exact-head review/CI, and post-merge verification.
