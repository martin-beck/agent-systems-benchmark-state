# AR-1191: Verified local-agent lifecycle

Acceptance criteria:

- only authenticated AR-1190 catalog entries can be selected;
- staging validates digest, signature, SBOM, license and provenance before activation;
- activation is atomic, idempotent and generation-bound, with bounded progress and durable status;
- cancel, retry, remove, reconnect and interruption recovery are safe and race-tested;
- failed self-tests never expose a partially active agent;
- exact-head review, signed DCO commit, CI and durable handoff evidence are complete.
