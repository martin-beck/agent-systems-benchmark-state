# AR-1044: Recover tmux foreground-group qualification

## Outcome

Restore deterministic standalone `asb-tui` trusted-main qualification after AR-1042's test harness
incorrectly equated tmux's pane process with the pane's foreground process-group leader. Cleanup
must target only a process group proven to be the exact pane TTY's foreground group at acquisition
and again immediately before signalling.

## Dependencies and ownership

Repository: `martin-beck/asb-tui` only. Owned paths are the tmux/screen PTY qualification helpers
and tests in `tests/terminal_foundation.rs`. Renderer behavior, application lifecycle, ASB source,
protocol semantics and public UI are out of scope. This is the bounded recovery for merged
`eb4960e816f2fe7cf0f01456655662d33618feb7` and failed trusted-main run 34550197610.
Branch: `fix/tmux-pane-foreground-group-recovery`. Worktree:
`agent-systems-benchmark-asb-tui-tmux-pane-foreground-group-recovery`.

## Trust and cleanup invariants

1. Construct the unique tmux server/socket RAII guard immediately after `new-session` succeeds,
   before any pane metadata or process lookup that can fail or panic. A guard without process-group
   authority must still remove its exact server/socket, but must never send a negative-PGID signal.
2. Query one bounded exact tmux pane identity containing `#{pane_pid}` and `#{pane_tty}`. Reject
   missing, multiple, malformed, relative, non-device or unexpected pane observations.
3. Derive the foreground group using a fixed, bounded `ps` query for the pane process's `tpgid`.
   Require numeric `pane_pid > 1` and `tpgid > 1`; reject zero, negative, the current test process
   group, and unrelated groups. Prove the candidate group is the foreground group for that exact
   pane TTY by checking bounded process metadata and matching canonical TTY identity. Do not require
   `pane_pid == tpgid`: hosted tmux may interpose a pane leader while the application owns the
   foreground group.
4. Store the accepted PID/TTY/foreground-PGID observation immutably in the session guard. Immediately
   before TERM, re-query the same unique tmux pane and process metadata and require the full tuple to
   equal the stored observation. A disappeared or transitioned tuple is already gone or is an
   ownership failure; a mismatch, lookup error, PID/PGID reuse indication or TTY transition must
   never trigger negative-PGID signalling.
5. After a validated match, perform bounded TERM then KILL only for that group and prove it gone.
   Independently and unconditionally terminate the exact unique tmux server and conditionally remove
   its exact socket. Normal explicit stop followed by `Drop` must remain harmless.

## Adversarial test matrix

- Accept a valid pane whose `pane_pid` differs from its positive foreground `tpgid`, when the exact
  pane TTY and group membership agree.
- Reject empty, oversized, non-UTF-8, extra-row, nonnumeric, zero, negative, PID 1, current-test
  group, missing TTY, non-device TTY and unrelated process-group observations.
- Reject any change to pane PID, pane TTY or foreground tpgid between acquisition and cleanup;
  verify no negative signal is attempted, while the unique tmux server/socket is still removed.
- Force metadata acquisition failure immediately after successful `new-session`; prove `Drop`
  cleans the exact server/socket and reports process-group cleanup unproven.
- Run a HUP-resistant pane child; prove a validated stable tuple receives bounded TERM/KILL and no
  exact worktree-binary process or owned socket remains.
- Keep the non-UTF-8 `Connection` capture, alternate-before/after transition and clean-config
  regressions from AR-1042 green.
