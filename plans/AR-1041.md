# AR-1041: Make Gemini hook readiness publication atomic

## Outcome

Remove a load-sensitive false `HookUnavailable` result from the Gemini process-boundary test without
weakening production hook discovery, time bounds, malformed-state rejection, or cleanup guarantees.

## Dependencies and ownership

Repository: `martin-beck/agent-systems-benchmark` only. Owned paths are the Gemini hook-readiness
waiter and its focused fixtures/tests in `crates/asb-agents`; unrelated adapter behavior, TUI code,
workflow policy and benchmark semantics are out of scope.
Branch: `fix/gemini-hook-readiness-race`. Worktree:
`agent-systems-benchmark-gemini-hook-readiness-race`.

## Work sequence

1. Preserve the hosted failure from PR #135 and reproduce it under bounded concurrent stress. Prove
   that `printf > action-count.ready` transiently exposes an empty file while the current waiter
   returns immediately for any readable value other than exact `ready\n`.
2. Define a closed readiness state machine: absent and incomplete publication retry only until the
   existing deadline; exact ready succeeds; stable malformed, oversized, wrong-type, symlink,
   replaced-owner, permission and I/O states fail closed with bounded cleanup.
3. Publish fixture readiness atomically through an owner-private same-directory temporary file,
   flush/close and rename, and remove redundant writers. Production code must not trust a path or
   payload that was not revalidated at the consumption boundary.
4. Add deterministic barriers that force reads before, during and after publication; cover empty,
   partial, malformed, oversized, delayed, never-ready, replacement and concurrent cases without
   timing-only sleeps as correctness evidence.
5. Run focused tests sequentially and under at least 24 concurrent workers, repeated default-parallel
   crate/workspace suites, full ASB quality/security/privacy gates and exact-head hosted CI.

## Acceptance criteria and evidence

The exact previously failing stress harness produces no false `HookUnavailable`; incomplete state
cannot be mistaken for malformed final state; malformed or hostile final state never becomes ready;
the deadline and cleanup remain bounded; production semantics are unchanged except for correct
publication observation. Require a signed+DCO commit, immutable independent review, exact-head CI
and post-merge main verification.
