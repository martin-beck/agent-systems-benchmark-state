acceptance criteria extend the shipped provider-selection and strict-replay architecture.

## Acceptance criteria

The document identifies exact researched revisions and primary sources; distinguishes mocks, strict
cassette replay, and local model execution; makes no compatibility or UI claim without tests; records
selection criteria and rejection conditions; covers offline/network, privacy, provenance, timing,
concurrency, tool calls, errors, cancellation, and independent grading; and links every recommended
implementation step to a dependency-ordered AR with owned paths, hostile tests, evidence limits, and
completion criteria.

## Evidence and completion

validation commands, immutable review, exact-head CI, and blockers. Do not merge without independent
review and do not implement runtime integration in this task.
