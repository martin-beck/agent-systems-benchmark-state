# AR-1420: Literature workload campaign integration

## Objective

Extend the existing multi-agent-by-workload campaign so every literature workload
that is actually qualified for local/mock or strict-replay execution can be selected
and benchmarked beside the built-in software-engineering suite.

## Dependencies and scope

- Depends on AR-1333 and the complete family work in AR-1417 through AR-1419.
- Product repository only; do not modify `asb-tui`.
- Owned paths: campaign matrix, tuple identity, evidence records, offline replay,
  report partitioning, docs, and tests.

## Acceptance

1. Derive the campaign matrix from the validated catalog, including built-in and
   literature IDs, capability constraints, agent compatibility, evidence class,
   task/scorer revision, and unavailable reasons. No hand-maintained omission list.
2. Run bounded tuples through deterministic local/mock or strict replay only, with
   per-tuple agent/workload/model/scorer/platform identity, attempt budget, reset,
   timeout, result, and content-addressed evidence. Never require a backend provider.
3. Keep repository repair, code-generation, terminal, interactive, reliability, and
   safety metrics partitioned; report pass^k and policy violations separately and
   refuse incomparable aggregate rankings.
4. Exercise interrupted tuples, duplicate scheduling, reset leakage, malformed tool
   calls, replay mismatch, unavailable/evaluator-only records, and network-denial
   paths. Add positive and negative tests for CLI, evidence, replay, and reports.
5. Verify focused/full gates, privacy/formal checks, exact-head CI, independent review,
   and every required post-merge assurance workflow before releasing the AR.

## Non-goals

No live provider, dataset download during a run, native platform claim, score
normalization across incompatible benchmark families, or TUI implementation.
