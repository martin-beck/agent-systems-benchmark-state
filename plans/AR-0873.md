# AR-0873: Generate CI workflow screenshots

## Outcome

Generate deterministic CLI and TUI example captures from real CI executions and embed them in the workflow guides with accessible text equivalents.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0807, AR-0872.
Owned paths: capture harness, pinned terminal renderer, public fixtures, `docs/workflows/assets/ci/`, alt text, provenance manifests, and capture CI.
Branch: `docs/ci-workflow-captures`. Worktree: `agent-systems-benchmark-ci-workflow-captures`.

## Work sequence

1. Execute the documented beginner, multi-agent/provider, monitoring, comparison, recording, and replay journeys in a deterministic pseudo-terminal against synthetic public fixtures.
2. Capture selected checkpoints as version-controlled SVG or PNG images plus complete linear text equivalents; never use staged mock screens.
3. Normalize terminal size, locale, theme, time, IDs, and public paths while retaining the exact release/commit and CI run provenance.
4. Redact and scan every frame, transcript, and artifact for credentials, private paths, prompts/responses, unstable identifiers, and accessibility failures.
5. Link each image to its generating scenario and verify that failed or skipped executions cannot publish apparently successful screenshots.

## Acceptance criteria

Every screenshot comes from a successful CI-executed workflow at the documented commit; images and text equivalents agree; captures are deterministic and privacy-safe; provenance is machine-readable; and stale or synthetic hand-drawn screenshots fail CI.

## Evidence and completion

Record tool and fixture digests, CI run IDs, generated-asset hashes, accessibility/privacy checks, immutable review, exact-head CI, and post-merge regeneration.
