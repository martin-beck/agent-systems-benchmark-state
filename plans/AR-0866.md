cross-builds, version output, or replay without the pinned live executable do not establish support.
Do not infer untested providers, models, plugins, autonomous permissions, platforms, architectures,
external services, or telemetry behavior. If official executable source/package/license closure or
any required live/replay evidence is unavailable, block this child with the exact reason; do not
substitute a compatible-looking SDK, mutable distribution, private credential, or weakened check.
The other agent series remains independent, and umbrella AR-0856 cannot complete until both final
qualification children are done.
