# AR-1305 plan: image-native user-session support

## Objective

Repair the pinned QEMU guest image so its supported `dbus-user-session` and
systemd user-manager path accepts `systemd-run --user` transient units. This is
the remaining infrastructure dependency for AR-1304 required containment.

## Scope

Guest root-image and seed provenance under `/srv/data/projects` only. Preserve
offline operation, `-net none`, no host mounts, fresh overlays and immutable
input digests. Do not weaken cgroup limits or alter product code, handoffctl,
or native-ARM requirements.

## Acceptance

- Package/service presence and user-manager ordering are proven in a fresh
  image; missing package or bus fails closed.
- A bounded transient probe succeeds with MemoryMax=3G, MemorySwapMax=3G,
  CPUQuota=200%, TasksMax=64, KillMode=control-group and RuntimeMaxSec=1800.
- Positive/negative image/seed tests and sanitized provenance pass; no raw
  logs or private paths are published.
- Signed SSH+DCO commits and exact-head independent review are recorded before
  AR-1304 retries `pr-publication`.
