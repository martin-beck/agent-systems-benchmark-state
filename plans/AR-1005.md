# AR-1005: Export interoperable privacy-safe traces

## Outcome

Expose stable causal ASB events and optional standards-based telemetry without binding storage to an evolving convention.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0101, AR-0104, AR-0502.
Owned paths: asb-protocol events, asb-store traces and exporter.
Branch: feature/trace-interoperability. Worktree: agent-systems-benchmark-trace-interoperability.
One worker owns this task; shared workspace/schema edits require coordinator integration.

## Work sequence

1. Define stable internal trace schema and a version-pinned optional OTLP projection.
2. Implement the smallest complete boundary and its independent failure tests.
3. Run focused and applicable full quality gates on the exact commit.
4. Publish a focused PR, reconcile CI and record verified limitations.

## Acceptance criteria

Causal run/trial/agent/tool/model IDs round-trip; OTLP mapping pins an OpenTelemetry GenAI semantic-convention revision; content capture is opt-in; secrets are removed; malformed spans and exporter backpressure cannot block/corrupt runs.

## Evidence and completion

Record source/tool versions, commands by digest, immutable commit and CI identifiers,
observed outcomes and remaining limitations. No raw private configuration or transcripts.
Include negative cases and a real production-boundary integration where applicable.
Do not satisfy native evidence with mocks or emulation. Finish only after the requested
outcome, review, exact-head checks and post-merge checks pass.
