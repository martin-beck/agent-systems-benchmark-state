# AR-1333: Multi-agent by workload benchmark campaign

## Outcome

A production/live campaign successor drives the full agent-by-workload matrix
under one pinned provider selection. The mandatory credential-free local/mock
campaign is owned by AR-1456; this AR must never block local development, CI,
or first-customer mock qualification on live provider access.

## Dependencies and ownership

AR-1456 owns the mandatory local/mock successor and depends on AR-1332,
AR-1433, and AR-1447. AR-1329 remains an optional production/live successor.
This AR owns only the later provider-backed campaign integration. Branch:
`feature/ar-1333-multi-agent-workload-campaign`. Worktree:
`agent-systems-benchmark-ar-1333-multi-agent-workload-campaign`.

## Work sequence

1. Define the optional production campaign command that expands the compatible
   agent matrix over the in-tree workload suite under one pinned provider
   selection, consuming runtime-owned live authority only after AR-1329 is
   complete.
2. For every tuple, persist intent, execute under explicit opt-in, capture and
   seal the cassette, and record agent, workload, model, digest, result, and
   replay outcome.
3. Make execute/progress/reconcile/cancel idempotent and restart-safe with
   exact provider, model, workload, and runtime identity matching.
4. Support offline replay of the complete sealed matrix; reject missing,
   duplicate, stale, or mismatched tuples.
5. Add production-boundary tests for full matrix, partial/interrupted campaign,
   idempotent restart, privacy scanning, and offline replay of the sealed
   matrix. Local/mock coverage belongs to AR-1456.

## Acceptance criteria

The campaign produces a per-tuple, digest-anchored evidence matrix for every
selected agent and workload; interrupted tuples never repeat uncertain paid
work; offline replay reproduces the matrix without provider contact; and no
aggregate summary can stand in for complete per-tuple coverage.

## Evidence and completion

Record immutable commits, matrix fixtures, per-tuple evidence, interruption and
replay results, independent review, exact-head CI, and post-merge verification.

## Current development and CI qualification boundary

The mandatory development and CI qualification path for this AR is a deterministic
local provider/LLM mock (LiteLLM-compatible where practical), including hostile
negative tests and offline replay where applicable. External/live OpenRouter or
other provider reachability is optional supplementary evidence only; it is never a
completion, dependency-readiness, or CI gate. Production egress policy, credential
non-disclosure, runtime-owned authority, namespace/relay attestation, cancellation
and teardown, and fail-closed denial of unapproved external traffic remain required
contracts. Existing live-provider dependency edges describe production integration
ordering only and must not be used to block local qualification or to claim external
reachability.
