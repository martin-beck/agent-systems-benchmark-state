# AR-1251: Add a bounded MockAgents transport sandbox fixture

## Outcome

Provide a credential-free, bounded transport/sandbox fixture that lets the real pinned MockAgents
executable be qualified for tool-result sequencing, cancellation/backpressure, outbound-network
denial, cleanup, and emulated AArch64 without relying on superficial HTTP assertions.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0888, AR-0889. AR-1250 supplies pinned platform and artifact evidence. Own only
ASB qualification fixtures, harness transport, and runner wiring; do not modify asb-tui or weaken gates.

## Acceptance

- Fixture is deterministic, offline, credential-free, bounded, and uses explicit pinned platform/
  runner identities and artifact hashes.
- Real executable evidence proves tool-result ordering, backpressure/cancellation terminal state,
  outbound-denial, malformed input, process-tree cleanup, repeat-clean-state, and arm64 QEMU runs.
- Hostile and failure cases fail closed with sanitized bounded evidence and no fallback/provider egress.
- Focused/full locked gates, independent review, signed-DCO exact-head CI, protected merge, and
  post-merge verification pass before release.
