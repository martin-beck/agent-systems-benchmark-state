# AR-0814: Secure remote enrollment and authorization

## Outcome

Provide understandable, auditable mutual trust between a controller TUI and benchmark runner with least-privilege authorization.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0813.
Owned paths: enrollment CLI/TUI, identity store, certificate issuance/import, roles/policy, rotation/revocation, and audit events.
Branch: `feature/remote-enrollment-authz`. Worktree: `agent-systems-benchmark-remote-enrollment-authz`.

## Work sequence

1. Implement explicit out-of-band fingerprint or one-time pairing with human-verifiable identities; never silently trust first use for mutation authority.
2. Define read-only observer, operator, and administrator permissions for status, launch, cancel, artifact metadata/access, configuration, enrollment, and revocation.
3. Store private keys through OS-protected secret references with mode/ownership checks; support short lifetimes, rotation, revocation, clock-skew handling, backup/restore rules, and break-glass recovery.
4. Bind every remote mutation to authenticated principal, role, request/idempotency ID, runner revision, and privacy-safe audit record.
5. Test stolen/expired/not-yet-valid/revoked/wrong-IP identities, role escalation, confused deputy, concurrent rotation, lost controller, compromised config permissions, and audit tampering.

## Acceptance criteria

Users can verify which runner and controller they trust; observer credentials cannot mutate; operator permissions are minimal and explicit; secrets never enter manifests/logs/history/exports; revocation takes effect predictably; certificate/key errors fail closed; and a runner remains locally recoverable without weakening remote trust.

## Evidence and completion

Record threat model, permission matrix, ceremony usability tests, cryptographic dependency pins, key-file audits using synthetic credentials, immutable commits, security review, CI, and recovery limits.
