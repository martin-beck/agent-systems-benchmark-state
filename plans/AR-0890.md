# AR-0890: Integrate a deterministic LLM double in CI

## Outcome

Provide a pinned credential-free network-denied CI service for protocol structural tests, with provenance/lifecycle proof preventing skipped or substituted servers from passing.

## Dependencies and ownership

Depends on AR-0888 and AR-0889. Own the selected lock/verifier, minimal launcher, CI job, fixtures and integration tests. Do not change live support, cassettes, scoring, CLI or TUI. Branch `ci/deterministic-llm-double`; worktree `agent-systems-benchmark-deterministic-llm-double-ci`.

## Work sequence

1. Adopt only the selected revision/artifact/license; verify digest before execution and prohibit moving tags/install pipes/network resolution/toolchain drift.
2. Start unprivileged on allocated loopback with resource/time bounds, identity readiness, unique state and no inherited credentials.
3. Deny DNS/provider egress for success, mismatch, bad URL and startup failure; never fall back to live or another mock.
4. Exercise buffered/SSE, tools, faults, concurrency, cancellation and teardown through the versioned synthetic contract with skip/no-op guards.
5. Retain compact public provenance, redact logs, terminate children and verify ports/state after all outcomes.
6. Pin actions/permissions, add license/security checks and label output protocol-only synthetic evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

Identity is verified; CI has no secret; egress denial is proven; wrong/missing/skipped/mismatched/leaking/stale states fail; repeated runs match; and catalogs remain unchanged.

## Evidence and completion

Record artifact/source digest, license, isolation proof, case inventory, repeat hashes, cleanup, security/privacy, full gates, immutable review, exact-head CI and post-merge runs.
