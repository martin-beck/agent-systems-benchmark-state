# AR-1260: Runtime-owned strict-replay integration

## Outcome

Wire the strict-replay CLI consumer to runtime-issued authority and the supervised sandbox
launch path. Execute a real bounded cassette request without forging readiness or permitting
provider egress.

## Dependencies and ownership

Depends on AR-1237, AR-1238, and AR-1239. AR-1248 is the superseded CLI-contract predecessor;
preserve its exact reviewed head and do not duplicate its provenance work.

Owned paths are `crates/asb-cli/src/replay_contract.rs`, `crates/asb-cli/src/lib.rs`, and
narrowly scoped CLI replay integration tests/fixtures. Do not modify asb-tui, provider catalogs,
campaign code, or the runtime supervisor/bundle implementations.

## Acceptance

- Consume only runtime-issued namespace attestation, `SandboxLaunchInput`, authenticated relay
  handoff, route/generation/deadline, command digests, and `ResourceLease`; stale or caller-forged
  values fail closed.
- `replay_plan` invokes `StrictReplayLaunchBridge::spawn` and retains lease/sidecar ownership for
  the complete bounded execution.
- Real cassette request/response succeeds through the authenticated loopback route; provider and
  descendant egress, malformed requests, missing credentials, and ambient configuration fail closed.
- Timeout, cancellation, crash, partial launch, restart, stale generation, duplicate attempt, and
  no-fallback cases reap the complete child tree and clean routes, sockets, leases, and state.
- Focused and full locked tests, fmt, Clippy, rustdoc, policy/privacy/formal/portability gates,
  SSH-signed DCO review, exact-head CI, protected merge, and post-merge verification pass.
- Public docs/schemas and the AR-1151 handoff are updated without private paths, credentials,
  child output, or raw logs.
