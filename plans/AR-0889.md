# AR-0889: Define the LLM fixture and scenario contract

## Outcome

Create a versioned, bounded, generated-public-data contract for deterministic protocol scenarios and evidence classification that coexists with, never replaces, the cassette schema.

## Dependencies and ownership

Depends on AR-0888. Own scenario schema/types, validators, generated fixtures, evidence labels, privacy scanner and contract tests. Coordinate shared result fields; do not alter cassette bytes or claim model-quality evidence. Branch `feat/llm-fixture-scenario-contract`; worktree `agent-systems-benchmark-llm-fixture-scenario-contract`.

## Work sequence

1. Translate tested candidates and AgentRR, agrepl, structural-testing and over-mocking findings linked by the research document into normative fields and limitations.
2. Specify matching, ordered/parallel sequences, SSE timing, tools, faults, cancellation and seed/fixture identity with canonical serialization and bounds.
3. Require `synthetic` origin and reject collision with `strict_replay`, `local_live` or `remote_live`; prohibit fallback/conversion.
4. Generate committed fixtures from public inputs; reject credentials, private hosts/paths, raw captures, prompt logs and unknown/unbounded fields.
5. Test hostile versions, ambiguous match, exhaustion, session leakage, traversal, invalid UTF-8, oversized streams and label downgrade.
6. Document authoring/review while retaining `asb-replay` as recorded-response authority.

## Acceptance criteria

Validation is fail closed; fixtures reproduce; sessions isolate cursors; privacy/bounds cover persisted fields; origins remain distinct in manifests/reports/comparisons; no fallback exists; and version migration is tested.

## Evidence and completion

Record schema fixture hashes, hostile/privacy tests, replay regressions, coverage/type/lint, immutable review, exact-head CI and post-merge compatibility.
