# AR-0521: Reproduce and pin the OpenHands replay environment

## Outcome
Provide independently reproducible, content-addressed OpenHands runtime provenance for replay qualification.

## Dependencies and ownership
Dependencies: AR-0309 and AR-0514 failure evidence. Owned paths are the OpenHands runtime manifest, verifier, fixtures, and documentation. No digest override is permitted.

## Acceptance criteria
Pin direct and transitive dependencies, licenses, interpreter and build inputs; publish a signed offline manifest and verifier; reproduce the approved digest from public inputs; reject altered inputs; and run focused plus applicable full quality and formal gates.

## Evidence
Record immutable refs, artifact hashes, SBOM/provenance, signatures, DCO, test results, and platform limits. Native replay remains prohibited until independent verification succeeds.
