# AR-1335: Credential-free CI stage for the benchmark path

## Outcome

A required CI stage exercises the complete benchmark path end to end against
loopback and synthetic doubles with no credentials, no egress, and no network,
keeping the unchanged 90% coverage floor and every native, formal, platform,
provenance, and privacy gate green.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-1333 (benchmark campaign) and AR-1334 (conformance and hostile
qualification). Owned paths: the benchmark-path CI stage definition, loopback
and synthetic-double harnesses, and the coverage/qualification integration in
the ASB product workflows. Branch: `feature/ar-1335-credential-free-benchmark-ci`.
Worktree: `agent-systems-benchmark-ar-1335-credential-free-benchmark-ci`.

## Work sequence

1. Define the required CI stage that runs provider selection, live-mode
   planning, execution with the deterministic double, capture, sealing, and
   strict offline replay on the benchmark path.
2. Include the hostile negative cases from AR-1334 in the stage so fail-closed
   behavior is continuously proven without any live provider.
3. Enforce that the stage carries no secrets, makes no paid calls, and performs
   no provider egress; deny and fail on any attempted network contact.
4. Integrate coverage so the new path does not lower the 90% floor, and keep
   all other gates unchanged.
5. Add CI tests: stage passes offline, stage fails on attempted egress or
   missing coverage, and the full benchmark path works under the double.

## Acceptance criteria

The benchmark path is exercised in required CI with zero credentials and zero
egress; coverage remains at or above the unchanged floor; hostile cases run
fail-closed; and no workflow change weakens any native, formal, platform,
provenance, or privacy gate.

## Evidence and completion

Record immutable commits, CI run evidence, coverage reports, egress-denial
proof, independent review, exact-head CI, and post-merge verification.
