# AR-1235: Portable Goose diagnostic fixture

## Outcome

Make the Goose diagnostic and executable-symlink negative fixture deterministic under the
emulated-AArch64 runner without weakening fail-closed executable, stderr, or lifecycle contracts.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependency: AR-0003. Repository: `martin-beck/agent-systems-benchmark`. Own only the Goose test
fixture and its focused portability coverage; do not change production launch semantics.

## Acceptance

- Reproduce and classify the AArch64 failure from post-merge run 35060286408, where the diagnostic
  fixture returned `GooseOutcome(status=Failed, exit_code=127)` instead of the expected error.
- Replace architecture-sensitive fixture assumptions with a deterministic repository-supported
  executable fixture, preserving the assertion that diagnostics fail closed and symlinked
  executables are rejected before execution.
- Add positive and negative tests for x86_64 and emulated AArch64, bounded output, cleanup, and
  privacy; do not skip or weaken the existing gate.
- Run focused, full locked, policy, portability, signature/DCO, and exact-head gates.

## Explicit non-goals

No production Goose behavior changes, native-host requirement, provider network, credentials,
TUI changes, or gate suppression.
