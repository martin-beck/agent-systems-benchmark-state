# AR-1013: Version measurement catalog semantics

## Outcome

Define ASB's authoritative, versioned measurement catalog and semantic taxonomy without owning any
frontend behavior.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0101 and AR-1001. Repository: `martin-beck/agent-systems-benchmark` only. Owned
paths are catalog Rust types, semantic grouping, validation, generated schema, bounded fixtures and
catalog documentation. No renderer, search, selection interaction, terminal lifecycle, Ratatui,
Crossterm, screen, widget, navigation or frontend state belongs here. Standalone UI consumption is
published through AR-1036 and implemented in `martin-beck/asb-tui` by AR-1014.

## Work sequence

Read the architecture, quality, formal-assurance, measurement, and CSB integration contracts. Define
the catalog schema and semantic taxonomy, add fixtures and negative tests, then obtain independent
schema/provenance review before serializing the workspace changes.

The baseline catalog depends on AR-0101 and AR-1001 only. Optional CSB monitoring from AR-0602 is a
later versioned extension, not a prerequisite: every unqualified CSB-derived measure must remain
absent or explicitly unsupported with provenance, and no UI may infer support from catalog presence.

## Acceptance criteria

Schema and Rust types are versioned; group and measurement IDs are stable; duplicate or ambiguous
names and incompatible units fail closed; aggregation, overhead, availability, live/replay support,
platform constraints, provenance and evidence limits are explicit; privacy-sensitive values cannot
enter public fixtures; unsupported CSB-derived measures are absent or explicitly unavailable; and
ASB contains no presentation or interaction behavior.

## Evidence

Record generated-schema parity, positive/maximal/empty fixtures, unknown/duplicate/unit-mismatch/
unsupported/privacy negatives, deterministic serialization and digest tests, full ASB quality and
privacy gates, independent schema/provenance review, exact-head CI and post-merge verification.
