# AR-0888: Spike deterministic LLM double conformance

## Outcome

Produce comparable OpenAI and Anthropic protocol, determinism, fault, privacy, and isolation evidence for every candidate in the research record, then recommend selection or rejection without adding a production adapter.

## Dependencies and ownership

Depends on AR-0879. Own a bounded spike harness, generated public fixtures, exact candidate lock/provenance manifest, result schema and evaluation report. Do not modify `asb-replay`, support catalogs, CLI/TUI or production launch paths. Branch `test/llm-double-conformance-spike`; worktree `agent-systems-benchmark-llm-double-conformance-spike`.

## Work sequence

1. Verify MockAgents v0.5.0, aimock v1.40.0, larsakerlund/llmock v0.1.2 and piyook/llm-mock 3.3.6 commits, licenses, digests, runtimes and primary links.
2. Exercise OpenAI Chat/Responses and Anthropic Messages; buffered/SSE responses; multiline data, usage, finish reasons, tools, parallel sessions, repeated fingerprints, cancellation and backpressure.
3. Add 4xx/5xx, disconnect, truncation, invalid JSON, delay, mismatch/exhaustion, late base-URL configuration, listener binding, DNS and outbound-provider hostile cases.
4. Run unprivileged with bounds, loopback plus outbound denial, no real credentials, and fixture/log/result privacy scans.
5. Repeat inputs to prove deterministic bytes/order where claimed. Record pass, fail, unsupported and untested, never translating missing behavior into pass.
6. Publish evidence/recommendation; keep third-party executables outside Cargo and retain Rust 1.93.

## Acceptance criteria

One public-input suite evaluates all exact candidates; no run contacts a provider; streaming/tool/fault/concurrency/cancellation assertions exist; pins/licenses/digests are retained; repeats verify claims; and no candidate enters support catalogs.

## Evidence and completion

Record source/artifact hashes, isolation proof, negative connect tests, privacy scans, repeat hashes, focused/full gates, immutable review, exact-head CI and post-merge evidence.
