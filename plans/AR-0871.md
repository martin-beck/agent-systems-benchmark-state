# AR-0871: Deliver record and replay user workflows

## Outcome

Provide executable workflows that record live LLM responses into an immutable redacted cassette and later select and strictly replay the exact compatible recording.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0314, AR-0502, AR-0503, AR-0505, AR-0869, AR-0870.
Owned paths: CLI/TUI recording and replay orchestration, source-selection help, workflow fixtures, privacy guards, and focused tests.
Branch: `feature/record-replay-user-workflows`. Worktree: `agent-systems-benchmark-record-replay-user-workflows`.

## Work sequence

1. Add explicit CLI and TUI actions for opt-in live recording, showing provider/cost/network consequences before execution.
2. Seal successful captures with format version, normalized compatibility identity, completeness, redaction result, integrity digest, and safe catalog metadata.
3. List only compatible recordings, require an exact cassette choice versus live execution, and display why near-matches are unavailable.
4. Run strict replay with provider network denied; prove agent/tool execution continues while responses come only from the pinned cassette.
5. Cover cancellation, partial/corrupt captures, mismatches, credentials, concurrent identical sessions, cleanup, recovery, and replay/live result labeling.

## Acceptance criteria

A beginner can record once and replay later in both interfaces without hidden steps; replay never contacts the provider; incomplete, corrupt, stale, or incompatible recordings fail closed; raw prompts/responses and secrets never enter public docs or CI artifacts; and live and replay evidence cannot be confused.

## Evidence and completion

Record sanitized synthetic fixtures, cassette digests, network-denial proof, command and interaction snapshots, immutable review, exact-head CI, and post-merge verification.
