# AR-1447: ASB local campaign qualification

## Objective

Qualify the complete credential-free ASB benchmark journey for first-customer
readiness: catalog-bound setup, deterministic local-mock run and sweep,
bounded campaign sealing, strict offline replay, report, and comparison.

## Boundaries

This AR never contacts a provider, requires a key, or fabricates live-provider
authority. External live capture remains in AR-1330 through AR-1333. The local
mock path must remain clearly labelled and network-denied. The broader
interactive wrapper enhancement AR-1338 remains separate.

## Acceptance

- The existing bounded `asb easy run` and `asb easy sweep` complete selected fixture workloads with
  the catalog-bound local mock and bounded evidence.
- A complete local campaign seals every selected tuple, rejects missing,
  duplicate, stale, or malformed entries, and becomes strictly replayable
  offline without provider fallback.
- Reports and comparisons validate manifests and refuse incompatible runs.
- Focused and full offline gates, hostile tests, and exact post-merge evidence
  pass; no credentials, prompts, responses, or private paths are persisted.
