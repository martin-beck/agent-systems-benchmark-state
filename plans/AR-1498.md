# AR-1498: Authenticated lifecycle artifact executor

## Outcome

Supply the ASB-owned runtime executor and activation authority required for the already-authenticated
renderer-neutral lifecycle router. Keep installation and activation transactional, owner-bound and
fail-closed; the standalone asb-tui remains a consumer only.

## Scope

- authenticated signed artifact acquisition/verification and target/libc compatibility;
- owner-only executor and activation boundary with generation/idempotency fencing;
- durable install, activation, cancellation, retry, status, remove and restart recovery;
- typed bounded errors and privacy-safe progress/status projections;
- protocol fixtures, clean-home and controlled-failure integration tests.

## Non-goals

No Ratatui/TUI rendering, arbitrary executable paths, ambient credential discovery, raw-key transport,
or weakening of existing `CapabilityUnavailable` behavior before the authority is complete.

## Dependencies

AR-1018, AR-1019, AR-1020, AR-1190, AR-1191 and completed AR-1496.

## Acceptance

The ASB lifecycle backend can prove signed artifact identity and compatibility, perform transactional
owner-bound activation, recover safely after interruption, and expose truthful status for all terminal
states. Exact protocol/schema fixtures and negative tests must pass with signed/DCO commits and
independent review before AR-1199 or asb-tui lifecycle work is reopened.
