# AR-0807: Qualify terminal frontend usability and isolation

## Outcome

Independently audit the completed TUI for usability, accessibility, failure isolation, resource interference, portability, installation, and upgrade safety.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-0702, AR-0804, AR-0805, AR-0806, AR-0812.
Owned paths: TUI end-to-end/fault/UX suites, packaging integration, operator documentation, and qualification evidence.
Branch: `test/tui-resilience-accessibility`. Worktree: `agent-systems-benchmark-tui-resilience-accessibility`.

## Work sequence

1. Test representative first-time and expert journeys: configure, validate, launch, monitor, reconnect, cancel, browse, repeat, compare, and export.
2. Exercise native x86_64 and pinned QEMU-emulated AArch64 mainstream Linux/openEuler userspace
   where images are available, plus local TTY, SSH, tmux, GNU screen, nested multiplexers, rapid
   resize, low color, no color, Unicode fallbacks, high latency, runner upgrades, and protocol skew.
3. Kill, hang, corrupt, overload, and restart the frontend/control connection while measuring runner correctness and performance isolation.
4. Audit keyboard navigation, contrast, focus, concise errors, help, destructive confirmations, privacy, dependency integrity, SBOM, and reproducible packaging.
5. Fix or create required follow-up ARs for every material defect; do not waive essential usability or isolation failures.

## Acceptance criteria

Independent users complete core journeys from documented installation; the frontend cannot change benchmark outcomes merely by attaching/detaching; failure recovery is deterministic; CPU/memory/I/O overhead is bounded and reported; accessibility fallbacks work; upgrades preserve compatible history; and unsupported terminals/platforms are explicit.

## Evidence and completion

Record test scripts, redacted observations, resource comparisons, immutable commits, reviews,
exact-head CI, emulated/native labels, package/SBOM/provenance, and post-install verification.
Native ARM64 terminal evidence is optional future qualification and cannot block this AR.
