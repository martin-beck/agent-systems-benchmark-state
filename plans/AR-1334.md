# AR-1334: OpenRouter free-model conformance and hostile qualification

## Outcome

The pinned OpenRouter free model is qualified through the AR-0315 conformance
harness across the compatible agent matrix, and hostile cases fail closed with
typed errors and no secret or uncertain provider effect. Real provider contact
is opt-in and never required for CI.

## Dependencies and ownership

Dependencies: AR-1327 (adapter projections and parity conformance) and AR-1328
(per-user configuration and enrollment). Owned paths: the conformance fixtures
for the pinned free model, hostile negative cases, rate-limit and timeout
handling, and the qualification evidence in the ASB product. Branch:
`feature/ar-1334-openrouter-conformance-qualification`. Worktree:
`agent-systems-benchmark-ar-1334-openrouter-conformance-qualification`.

## Work sequence

1. Run the exact free-model snapshot through the AR-0315 conformance harness
   over every compatible agent with identical effective projections.
2. Exercise hostile cases: absent credentials, credential bleed, endpoint
   identity confusion, rate limiting, timeouts, streaming and tool-call
   divergence, malformed responses, and replay/live confusion.
3. Prove every negative case fails closed with typed errors, no secret in
   diagnostics, and no uncertain provider effect.
4. Confirm the qualification uses loopback and synthetic doubles for automation;
   a real provider is used only under explicit opt-in and recorded separately.
5. Publish the qualification result and evidence limits, keeping the support
   matrix truthful.

## Acceptance criteria

Every conformance and hostile case has a real pinned-boundary test with exact
evidence; identical means all requested and observable effective fields match;
unsupported settings remain unsupported; no secret leaks; and offline CI needs
no provider or key.

## Evidence and completion

Record immutable commits, conformance fixtures, hostile-case results, redacted
evidence, independent review, exact-head CI, and post-merge verification.
